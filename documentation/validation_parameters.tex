\section{Validity Threats}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Silent IDE use} --- inline completions (Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf) leave no detectable signal.
  \item \textbf{Public repos only} --- enterprise and private usage is invisible.
  \item \textbf{Location inference bias} --- $\approx$40--45\% of owners unresolved.
  \item \textbf{Creation-date filter} --- repos created before 24 Feb 2025 excluded.
  \item \textbf{AGENTS.md ambiguity} --- multi-vendor file; classified as \texttt{Generic}, does not affect adoption counts.
  \item \textbf{Multi-agent attribution} --- a repo with 2+ agents may reflect team diversity or tool migration, not individual multi-tool use.
\end{itemize}
